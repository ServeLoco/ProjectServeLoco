Account and Data Deletion

VillKro Private Limited · App: VillKro (com.yashsiwach.villkro) · Last updated 25 September 2026

In short. Open the application and go to Profile → Delete Account. Your account closes and you are signed out straight away. A thirty-day period then follows during which you can change your mind, after which everything is erased automatically. If you have ever placed an order, the commercial record of that order is kept for tax law but is stripped of everything that identifies you.

The rest of this page explains each of those steps in full, including exactly what is erased, what is not, and why.

1. How to delete your account

There are two routes. The first is the normal one and takes a few seconds. The second exists for the case where you can no longer get into the application at all.

Route A — from inside the application

  1. Open the VillKro application and sign in.
  2. Tap the Profile tab at the bottom right of the screen.
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the Profile screen and tap Delete Account.
  4. Read the confirmation screen, which restates what is about to happen, and confirm.

You are signed out on every device immediately and the account is scheduled for erasure. You do not need to contact us, you do not need to give a reason, and nobody will telephone you to talk you out of it.

Route B — by e-mail, if you cannot reach the application

If you have lost access to your phone or can no longer sign in, send a request from the e-mail address registered on your VillKro account to:

decodelabsofficial@gmail.com

Use the subject line Delete my VillKro account, and include your registered mobile number in the body of the message so that we can confirm the request is genuinely yours. This verification step is not bureaucracy for its own sake: without it, anyone who learned your e-mail address could have your account destroyed.

We will acknowledge your request within 24 hours and complete the deletion within 30 days.

2. What happens, and when

Deletion is not instantaneous, and we want to be transparent about the timeline rather than let you assume the data vanishes the moment you tap the button.

WhenWhat happens
Immediately Your account is marked for deletion and you are signed out on every device where you were logged in.
Days 1 to 30 — the grace period The decision can still be reversed. Sign in and choose Cancel deletion in your Profile, and nothing will have been lost. This period exists deliberately: deletion is permanent and irreversible, and an accidental tap, or a deletion made under pressure from someone else, ought to be recoverable. We judged the ability to change your mind to be worth more than thirty days of earlier erasure.
After 30 days An automated job, which runs once every day, permanently erases the account. No human intervention is required and none can stop it once the period has elapsed.
Within a further 90 days Encrypted backups that still contain a copy of the old data are rotated out and destroyed in the ordinary backup cycle.

3. What is erased

The following are removed permanently and cannot be recovered afterwards:

  • Your name.
  • Your mobile telephone number, and the Firebase sign-in identity linked to it.
  • Your password hash, if you had set a password.
  • Your WhatsApp number, if you had provided one.
  • Every delivery address you had saved, together with the map pins and any landmark notes attached to them.
  • The contents of your cart.
  • Your push notification tokens. Notifications stop reaching your device.
  • Your active sessions and sign-in tokens, so that no existing login anywhere remains usable.
  • Any pending password reset requests.

If you never placed an order, that is the whole story. Your user record is deleted outright and nothing identifying you remains in our systems.

4. What is kept, and why

If you placed at least one order, the account record is anonymised rather than deleted outright. We would rather explain this clearly here than have you discover it later.

The reason is that Indian tax and company law requires a business to retain its sales and invoice records for a number of years, and an order record cannot simply be detached from the customer it belongs to without destroying the accounting record itself. Deleting the row outright is therefore not something we are permitted to do.

What we do instead is remove everything from that record that identifies you as a person. Your name is replaced with “Deleted User”. Your telephone number is replaced with a meaningless internal reference that cannot be dialled and corresponds to nothing. Your password hash, sign-in identity, WhatsApp number, saved addresses and push tokens are erased entirely, exactly as described in section 3.

What survives is the commercial fact of the order: what was bought, from which shop, for how much, and on what date. It is no longer attached to anything that identifies you. It cannot be used to contact you, to market to you, or to recognise you if you ever create a new account. It exists solely so that the company's books balance and can be audited.

5. Things worth knowing before you delete

  • Deal with any live order first. Deleting your account does not automatically cancel an order that a shop has already accepted and begun preparing. If you have an order in progress, either wait for it to arrive or cancel it before deleting, so that nobody is left holding food for a customer who can no longer be reached.
  • After the thirty days, this cannot be undone. There is no recovery process, no backup we can restore on request, and no way for our support team to bring the account back. That is by design: a deletion that could be reversed by anyone who contacted support would not be a real deletion.
  • If you only want a break, sign out instead. Signing out leaves everything exactly as it is and you can return whenever you like.
  • Deleting the application from your phone is not the same as deleting your account. Uninstalling removes the software from your device but leaves your account and its data on our servers. Use Profile → Delete Account if that is what you want.

6. Your other rights over your data

Deletion is one of several rights the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 gives you. You are also entitled to obtain a summary of the data we hold about you and of what we do with it, to have inaccurate data corrected or incomplete data completed, to withdraw a consent you previously gave, and to nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf if you die or become unable to act.

Each of these is explained, with the steps for exercising it, in section 12 of the Privacy Policy.
