Ordering in Agroha, step by step

  1. Install VillKro and sign in. Get the free app from Google Play or the App Store and sign in with your phone number.
  2. Set your address in Agroha. Pick your exact delivery location. Save it once and reuse it next time.
  3. Choose an open shop. Browse the shops that deliver to you, add items to your cart and adjust quantities.
  4. Check the total and payment. Review prices and any charges, then choose UPI or cash on delivery where it is offered.
  5. Track your rider. Get a notification at each step and watch your rider on the map until the order arrives.

Hours, payment and delivery time

You can order daily from 9 AM to 11 PM (IST). Each shop sets its own hours and may close for a while; the app shows which shops are open right now.

Payment options appear at checkout. UPI is available, and cash on delivery may be offered depending on your area and the time of the order. Downloading VillKro is free.

How long delivery takes depends on how quickly the shop packs your order, how far you are and rider availability. The live status in the app is the best guide.
