How delivery works here

  1. Set your location near Gorakhpur. Choose your exact delivery address in the VillKro app.
  2. Choose an available shop. Browse its products and add the items you need to your cart.
  3. Review the total. Check item prices, delivery charges and the payment options shown before confirming.
  4. Follow your order. Get order updates and track your rider in the app as your order comes to you.

Ordering hours, payments and delivery time

Ordering hours: 9 AM–11 PM daily, Indian Standard Time. Individual shops may have different hours or be temporarily unavailable. Check the app before ordering.

Payments: use UPI or cash on delivery where offered at checkout. Cash availability can depend on your area and ordering time. The app is free to download; your checkout shows the total and any applicable charges.

Delivery time depends on shop preparation, distance and rider availability. Follow the order status and rider tracking for updates.

Does VillKro deliver to my village or address?

Enter your exact delivery location in VillKro to check coverage for your village, street or home. Available shops and products are shown for the address you select.

If no shops appear, check that your location is correct. Your address may be outside current coverage, or shops may be unavailable at that time.
