How to place your first order

  1. Download and sign in. Use the Google Play or App Store link above, then open VillKro.
  2. Set your location. Check which local shops deliver to your address. Availability depends on your delivery area.
  3. Choose your items. Browse available products, add them to your cart and review your order before placing it.
  4. Follow your delivery. Check order updates and use live rider tracking in the app.

Where VillKro delivers

VillKro serves Gorakhpur and Agroha in Hisar district, Haryana. Shops, products and delivery availability depend on your location, and a shop may be closed or temporarily unavailable. The app shows your current options after you set your location.
