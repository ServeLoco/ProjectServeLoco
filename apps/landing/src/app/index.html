Local groceries and food, ordered from your phone.

VillKro connects you with nearby shops for groceries, food, dairy and daily essentials. Set your delivery location in the app to see the shops and products available to you.

The app is free to download. Your order total and any applicable delivery charges are shown during checkout.

Local shopping for villages and small towns

VillKro brings neighborhood shopping to your phone, with local riders handling delivery. Browse groceries, fresh produce, prepared meals and household essentials. Pharmacy items depend on participating shops and local availability.

  • Order updates. Follow confirmation, preparation and delivery, with notifications in the app.
  • Payment choices. Use UPI or cash on delivery where available. Cash may be unavailable for some areas or ordering times; check your options at checkout.

How to place your first order

  1. Download and sign in. Use the Google Play or App Store link above, then open VillKro.
  2. Set your location. Check which local shops deliver to your address. Availability depends on your delivery area.
  3. Choose your items. Browse available products, add them to your cart and review your order before placing it.
  4. Follow your delivery. Check order updates and use live rider tracking in the app.

Check delivery availability before ordering

Shops, products and delivery availability depend on your location. A shop may also be closed or temporarily unavailable. The app shows your current options after you set your location.

Delivery time depends on shop preparation, distance and rider availability. Follow your order status in VillKro for updates.
