FAQ

Questions? Answers.

What is VillKro?
VillKro is a delivery app for your town. You order from local shops near you — groceries, food, dairy and daily essentials — and a rider brings it to your door.
Where does VillKro deliver?
We are live in Gorakhpur, Haryana. Open the app and set your location to see the shops that deliver to you.
How do I pay?
Pay the rider when your order arrives. There is no subscription and no fee to use the app.
How fast is delivery?
Most orders arrive within 30–45 minutes. You can track your rider live in the app the whole way.
Who can use VillKro?
VillKro is for people aged 18 and above.
How do I delete my account?
You can delete your account from the app at any time. See account & data deletion for details.
